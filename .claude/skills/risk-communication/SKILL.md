# Risk communication

- Lead with one sentence: level + when + main reason with a number. "Flood risk tomorrow: High — 4–6 in of rain on already wet ground, high tide 7:42 pm."
- Use the word, an icon and a colour for each level — never colour alone.
- Official alerts first and verbatim headline: "NWS: Flash Flood Warning until 9:00 pm".
- Always visible on flood screens: "Flood Watch is not an official warning service. Follow the National Weather Service and local emergency officials."
- Say what we don't know: "Street-level flooding depends on local drainage we can't see."
- Calm, direct, no jokes or hype; 8th-grade reading level; US units with a toggle.
- Action hints only from official sources (NWS: "Turn Around, Don't Drown").
